 Project Name
  Sari-Sari Finance

  One-Line Description
  A community-driven micro-inventory financing platform that provides sari-sari store owners with short-term capital  for bulk inventory through Soroban smart contracts.

  Track
  Track 2: Financial Inclusion & Everyday Payments (also applies to Track 5: Social Impact)

  Problem It Solves
  Sari-sari store owners often lack the upfront capital required to purchase inventory in bulk, forcing them to buy
  in smaller quantities at higher unit prices. This "poverty premium" reduces their profit margins and hinders
  business growth. Sari-Sari Finance connects these owners with local community lenders who can provide instant,
  short-term liquidity to unlock bulk-purchase discounts.

  How It Uses Stellar
   - Soroban Smart Contracts: Manages the entire loan lifecycle (Request → Funding → Repayment) trustlessly
     on-chain.
   - Stellar Asset Contract (SAC) Ready: Designed to utilize USDC or local stablecoins for seamless fund
     disbursement and repayment.
   - Low-Cost Transactions: Leverages Stellar’s sub-cent fees to make daily micro-repayments (as small as 50-100
     PHP) economically viable for small store owners.

  Novelty Note (optional, for bonus points)
  While micro-lending exists, Sari-Sari Finance focuses specifically on Inventory Financing with a "community-first"  lending model. Unlike traditional apps that require credit scores, our system allows for "neighborhood trust" to
  be codified on-chain. We’ve also implemented a dual-role dashboard that allows any community member to act as both  a store owner and a lender, fostering a circular local economy.
